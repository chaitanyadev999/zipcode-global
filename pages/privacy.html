Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1

Information We Collect

PO ZipCode Global is designed with privacy at its core. We believe in minimal data collection:

  • We do NOT collect personal information. No names, emails, or phone numbers are stored on our servers.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking. There are no third-party tracking cookies on this site.
  • We do not store search queries. Your postal code lookups are processed in real-time and never saved.
  • No accounts required. You can use every feature without creating an account or logging in.
  • Map data is provided by OpenStreetMap/Leaflet. Map tiles are handled by open-source services under their own privacy policies.
2

How We Use Data

All postal code data displayed on PO ZipCode Global is sourced from public government databases:

  • India data: data.gov.in — Government of India Open Data portal (India Post)
  • USA data: USPS public postal data — United States Postal Service
  • World data: Various national government postal services across 121 countries

Data is displayed as-is for informational purposes only. We do not modify, sell, or redistribute the source data for commercial gain.

3

Third-Party Services

PO ZipCode Global integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — Font loading for typography (Inter, Space Grotesk)
  • OpenStreetMap / Leaflet.js — Interactive map display and geocoding
  • flagcdn.com — Country flag images (no personal data sent)
  • jsDelivr CDN / GitHub — Postal code dataset hosting and delivery
  • Google AdSense — Advertising (if enabled; operates under Google's own privacy policy)
Note: Each third-party service operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their respective policies for details on how they handle data.
4

Cookies

We take a minimal approach to cookies:

  • We use only essential cookies required for basic site functionality (such as remembering your region filter preference).
  • No tracking or analytics cookies are used without your explicit consent.
  • If Google AdSense is active, it may set its own cookies in accordance with Google's advertising policies.
  • You can clear cookies at any time via your browser settings without affecting site functionality.
5

Data Accuracy & Disclaimer

While we strive to provide accurate postal code information:

  • We cannot guarantee 100% correctness of all data displayed across 121 countries.
  • If you find incorrect data, please use our Report a Mistake page.
  • Data is updated periodically from the original source government databases.
  • Postal codes, location names, and administrative boundaries may change over time.

Always verify critical information with official postal service sources for important business or logistics decisions.

6

Children's Privacy

PO ZipCode Global is committed to protecting younger users:

  • Our service is not directed to children under 13 years of age.
  • We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.
  • If we become aware that a child has provided personal information, we will take steps to delete it promptly.
7

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for legal compliance.

  • All changes will be posted directly on this page.
  • The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised whenever changes are made.
  • We encourage you to review this page occasionally to stay informed.
8

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or feedback about this privacy policy or our data practices:

  • Report Mistakes: Use our Report page to flag incorrect postal code data.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1

Information We Collect

PO ZipCode Global is designed with privacy at its core. We believe in minimal data collection:

  • We do NOT collect personal information. No names, emails, or phone numbers are stored on our servers.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking. There are no third-party tracking cookies on this site.
  • We do not store search queries. Your postal code lookups are processed in real-time and never saved.
  • Map data is provided by OpenStreetMap. Map tiles and geocoding are handled by a third-party open-source service.
2

How We Use Data

All postal code data displayed on PO ZipCode Global is sourced from public government databases:

  • India data: data.gov.in — Government of India Open Data portal
  • USA data: USPS public postal data — United States Postal Service
  • World data: Various government postal services across 121 countries

Data is displayed as-is for informational purposes only. We do not modify, sell, or redistribute the source data.

3

Third-Party Services

PO ZipCode Global integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — Font loading for typography (Inter, Space Grotesk)
  • OpenStreetMap / Leaflet — Map display and geocoding via the Leaflet.js library
  • GitHub — Data hosting via raw.githubusercontent.com for postal code datasets
  • Google AdSense — Advertising delivery (if enabled by the site owner)
Note: Each third-party service operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their respective policies for details on how they handle data.
4

Cookies

We take a minimal approach to cookies:

  • We use only essential cookies required for basic site functionality.
  • No tracking or analytics cookies are used without your explicit consent.
  • If Google AdSense is active, it may set its own cookies in accordance with Google's advertising policies.
5

Data Accuracy

While we strive to provide accurate postal code information:

  • We cannot guarantee 100% correctness of all data displayed across 121 countries.
  • If you find incorrect data, please use our Report page or email us directly.
  • Data is updated periodically from the original source government databases.

Postal codes, location names, and administrative boundaries may change over time. Always verify critical information with official sources.

6

Children's Privacy

PO ZipCode Global is committed to protecting younger users:

  • Our service is not directed to children under 13 years of age.
  • We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.
  • If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.
